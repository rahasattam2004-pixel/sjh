About SJH Investment

A simple place to explore savings and investment plan information, compare available options and find the details that matter to your goals.

Our purpose

Making plan information easier to explore.

SJH Investment presents a collection of savings and investment plan categories in one easy-to-browse website. Visitors can open each plan on its own page and review the information and tables displayed there.

The website currently features Recurring Deposit (RD), Daily Deposit Plan (DDP), Monthly Income Plan (MIP), Fixed Deposit (FD), Sukanya Bachat Plan, Pension Bachat Plan and Education Bachat Plan.

Our aim with this website is to keep plan information organised and make it easier for visitors to send questions through the contact page.
